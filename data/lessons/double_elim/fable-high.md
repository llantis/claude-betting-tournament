# Lessons: fable-high (double elimination)
_Written after round 2 (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-23)._

## Where I went wrong
- 10 bets, 4-6, -$4.00: stated 54% confidence, won 40%. No demonstrated edge over the market.
- Totals went 2-4 for -$3.70 (93% of my losses). The three biggest losses were NBA totals at 1.88-1.94, bet on "edges" of 0.5-1.5 points from half-point book differences or 1-2c Kalshi/Polymarket gaps. That is noise, not edge.
- Round 1 went 0-2 on story bets ("offense is broken", "last in scoring") at 58% and 53%.
- All-in bets at 1.88-1.91 went 1-2, and both eliminations came there (JMU -19.5, Over 228.5). Once I admitted the pick was below break-even and bet it anyway.
- Public-ticket percentages, injuries and form are already in the price.

## What worked
- Pricing off multi-book plus Kalshi/Polymarket consensus: 4-4 in round 2, against 0-2 for stories in round 1.
- Taking the shortest price on an all-in day (Vegas ML 1.80) won; one clear stale-line bet (market moved 1-2 points past the slate number) won. Tiny samples: hints only.
- Field-wide hint: totals 7-11, spreads 18-14, prices 1.96 or longer 7-15.

## Rules for my future bets
1. Survival comes first. Expected days alive is about 6.0 at a 50% win rate and 6.9 at 54%, so maximise win probability, not payout.
2. Each day, first shortlist every option priced 1.80-1.85. Pick the one with the highest no-vig consensus win probability (at least 3 books plus Kalshi/Polymarket on the exact line).
3. Go above 1.85 only if nothing at 1.85 or shorter exists, or if rule 5 applies. Never take 1.96 or longer.
4. After a loss (all-in), and on day 1 of a round: moneyline or spread only, never a total. Take the highest consensus win probability at 1.80-1.85, even if slightly below break-even.
5. The only edge I may claim is a stale slate line: current consensus at 3 or more books has moved at least 1 full point (NBA/NFL/NCAAF) or 5c on a moneyline past the slate price in my favour, re-checked just before answering. Half-point or 1-2c gaps count as zero.
6. Totals only under rule 5, and only after a win. If a total ties with a moneyline or spread, take the moneyline or spread.
7. Confidence equals the no-vig consensus probability for my exact line. Add nothing for narrative. Cap at 55%, or 56% with a rule-5 stale line. If I write "nothing is +EV", confidence must be below 1/price.
8. Bet only games where I can find that consensus; skip leagues where I can't.
9. Always list two backup picks that follow the same rules.
