# Lessons: fable-high (bankroll)
_Written after round 1 (2026-10-22 to 2026-11-21)._

## Where I went wrong
- Out on day 12 of 31 at $0; never above the $10 start. All four players finished at $0, so simply surviving with any money would have won.
- Sizing killed me, not just picks. I staked 54-100% of bankroll every day, ~3 bets/day. Bets at exactly $1.00: 17 bets, -$0.65. Bets above $1.00: 19 bets, 7-12, -$9.35. My biggest stake of the day went 4-8.
- Two days did it: 10-23 (staked $7.00 of $9.60, 1-4, -$5.02) and 10-26 (staked $5.60 of $8.60, 1-3, -$3.54). After that every $1 minimum bet was 20-35% of my bankroll.
- Overconfident: said 55%, won 44%, prices implied 53%. My 55-59% band went 6-12. I added 2-3 points to implied on every bet with no evidence I can beat the market.
- Underdogs at 2.00+: 0-4, -$4.15, all "favorite has a weak goalie / is in crisis" stories. My last $1.15 went on a dog I called "roughly fair".
- "Stale line / better than consensus" reasons: about 7-11, -$8.14, and I staked most on them. NHL moneylines 3-5, -$3.76.

## What worked
- Favorites under 1.70: 4-0, +$2.88. Only 4 bets: a hint, not proof.
- $1.00 bets roughly broke even. Small stakes kept me alive; nothing else did.

## Rules for my future bets
1. Place exactly ONE bet per day, staked exactly the minimum ($1.00). Never zero (forfeit), never more.
2. Only exception: if bankroll is $15 or more, total daily stake may reach 8% of bankroll, across at most 2 bets.
3. Never stake above the minimum because a bet "feels stronger". My bigger stakes lost more often.
4. No bets priced 2.00 or higher. Ever, including when short-stacked.
5. Default pick: a favorite priced 1.40-1.80 with one verified, concrete fact (confirmed starting goalie/QB, confirmed absence, rest gap). No such fact: take the shortest-priced favorite in that range anyway.
6. Confidence = 1/price minus 0.02. Add at most 0.02 for a verified fact. Never state above 0.58 on a price of 1.80 or higher.
7. A line that differs from consensus or a model site's percentage is not, alone, a reason to bet or to raise confidence.
8. Avoid NHL moneylines priced 1.80+ and NFL sides/totals until my record there is positive over 20+ bets.
9. If bankroll is under $3, pick the lowest-priced favorite at 1.30 or above; survival beats payout.
10. Before submitting, check: one bet, $1.00, price under 2.00, confidence within 2 points of implied.
