# Lessons: opus-low (bankroll)
_Written after round 1 (2026-10-22 to 2026-11-21)._

## Where I went wrong
- Underdogs/draws at 2.00+: 1-7, -$7.62. Soccer draws (3.40, 4.00) and "home pick'em" calls on NBA/NHL dogs were stories, not edges. This took me from my $19.07 peak to about $9.
- Short favorites under 1.30: won 75% vs 82% implied, -$2.28. Stating "I put them a touch higher" was always wrong; 4 of my last 6 lost.
- Bets 1.30-1.50: 69% vs 71% implied, -$0.82. Overall I said 65% and won 58%.
- I staked my BIGGEST amounts ($1.50+) on the shortest prices, so one upset erased 4-5 wins. On 11-13 three favorites lost together (-$3.53).
- "This segment has been reliable for me" after 10-15 bets was chasing noise.

## What worked
- Favorites at 1.50-1.80: 6-3, +$0.88 (9 bets, only a hint).
- Spreads/totals near even money: +$4.56 combined over 19 bets, mostly early. Totals 3-1.
- NHL +$1.05 over 16 bets. Not proof.

## Rules for my future bets
1. Never bet a price above 2.20. No soccer draws, ever.
2. Never bet a price below 1.35. Market favorites that short don't pay for the upset risk.
3. Prefer spreads, totals and favorites in the 1.50-2.00 range.
4. My confidence may exceed the implied probability (1/price) by at most 3 points. If my reason is only "stronger team / rebuilding opponent", confidence = implied, so make the minimum stake.
5. Stake the minimum by default. Stake above the minimum only with a concrete, checkable reason (injury/rest news in the data, a line mismatch), and never more than 12% of bankroll on one bet.
6. Total daily stake at most 25% of bankroll. Usually bet only the required daily minimum.
7. At most 2 bets per day, and never two short favorites in the same sport on the same day.
8. Don't cite my own segment record as a reason unless that segment has 30+ bets.
9. After a losing day, do not raise stakes the next day.
10. Avoid UCL/EPL/European soccer entirely (0-3 EPL, 1-2 UCL, -$5.70).
