# Lessons: sonnet-low (double elimination)
_Written after round 2 (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-23)._

## Where I went wrong
- Round 2 ended after 2 bets: both were NBA double-digit road underdog spreads (Wizards +10.5, Bucks +15.5, about 1.92). Both lost. My only reason was "big spreads are inflated", which I never checked.
- Overall record is 5-6 with ROI -17%. I averaged 53% confidence and won 45%; the prices implied 52%. I claimed edges of 1 to 3 points that I never verified.
- NBA is 1-3 for me. Moneyline is 1-4. I once wrote "I did not research injuries or form" and bet anyway.
- I made the same generic bet on day 1 and day 2, so the day-1 loss told me nothing and a second loss ended the round.

## What worked
- Spreads are 4-2 and large NCAAF/NFL underdogs are 3-0. That is only 6 bets, so it is a hint, not proof.
- Confidence of 50-54% was fine (62% win rate, but only 8 bets). Confidence of 55% or more was 0-2.

## Rules for my future bets
1. Do not bet an NBA spread of +8 or more on the "inflated spread" argument alone. Only bet it if a web search shows a specific, named reason, such as a star out for the favorite or a back-to-back.
2. Before every bet, search the web for injuries, lineups and rest. The reason I write must cite at least one concrete fact from that search. If I can't find one, pick the option closest to a fair price.
3. Cap confidence at 55%. Set confidence no higher than the market-implied probability (1/price) plus 2 points unless I cite a specific fact.
4. After a loss (full-bankroll stake), do not take an underdog above 2.00 or a spread of more than 8 points. Pick a clear favorite at 1.80 to 1.95 on a moneyline or short spread, with lineups confirmed.
5. After a win (half-bankroll stake), I may take a researched spread of 1.90 to 2.00 in NCAAF or NFL, where my sample is positive.
6. Avoid moneyline favorites priced 1.80 to 1.85 in near pick'em games. The Knicks at 1.80 and Utah at 1.82 both lost, and I had no edge.
7. Never choose an underdog moneyline at 2.10 or longer. My one attempt (Newcastle at 2.15, not researched) lost.
