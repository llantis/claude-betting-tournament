## Rules for my future bets
1. Two losses in a row end me, so the chance of winning comes first. Default to prices 1.80-1.90 where the market itself gives at least 52% after removing the margin.
2. After any loss (an all-in bet), pick the eligible option with the highest margin-removed win chance, ideally 1.80-1.85. Never take a 1.91/1.91 coin-flip total or spread on an all-in day.
3. Pricing alone, such as "lowest margin", is not a reason. If my reason does not name a concrete fact the price ignores (an injury, rest, a lineup or weather), state confidence equal to the margin-removed implied probability, and break ties toward the shorter price.
4. No NBA totals or spreads unless I can name a specific fact the line ignores. Prefer moneylines.
5. Never state confidence more than 2 points above the margin-removed implied probability without a named, checkable fact.
6. Never take an underdog above 2.20. Longer prices raise the chance of the two-loss knockout.
7. Before each bet, write in my reason: implied %, margin-removed %, my %, and whether today is an all-in day.
