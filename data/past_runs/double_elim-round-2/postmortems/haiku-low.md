**Where it went wrong**
- 5 of 7 bets were moneyline underdogs or thin-edge sides priced 2.00 or higher. Underdogs at 2.00+ went 1-4 (ROI -64%), and that one win came at 2.24.
- Every losing bet's reason was a story: "thin edge," "small edge from home court," "no injury or form research done." None cited a verified injury, goalie, rest or form fact. The stake is set by the rules, so the pick and price are the only levers, and these picks had no basis beyond the home-ice prior.
- The knockout run is the clearest lesson. After the Pittsburgh-to-Cleveland losses, the bankroll was all-in and the next two picks were 2.42 and 2.85 longshots (Toronto, Vancouver). Both lost and ended the round. Those were the least likely bets to win at the point where one loss ended everything.
- NHL 2-3 and NBA 0-2. Moneyline 2-4 (ROI -41%).

**Calibration**
- Stated average confidence 46%, actual win rate 29%, market-implied 45%. Overconfident by about 17 points.
- Bets at stated confidence under 50% won 25%. At 50-54% they won 0% (2 bets). Only one bet at 55-59% won.
- The confidence numbers were set near the implied price, not from evidence, so they carried no information. Those numbers should have been capped at the market price.

**What worked**
- Only the two wins: Winnipeg ML at 2.24 and Vegas ML at 1.80 (home favorite). Both were priced at or below 2.24 and both were home teams.
- Favorites under 2.00 went 1-1. Too few bets (7 total) to trust any segment. Under about 20 bets this is a hint only.

**Rules for my future bets**
- Do not pick a moneyline or spread priced above 2.20 (underdogs above 2.20 went 0-4 in this competition's sample for me).
- Do not take a pick without a specific, checkable reason in the reason field: a named injury, goalie or lineup change, rest or travel situation, or a recent form number. "Home ice" and "thin edge" alone are not reasons.
- Set confidence no higher than the implied probability from the price, unless the reason names one verified fact that moves it. Never state a confidence above 55% without that fact.
- After a loss (bankroll all-in, one more loss ends the round), pick the strongest favorite priced 1.80 to 2.00 with a verified edge. Do not take a longshot to recover.
- Before each bet, check the report card: if the last three bets in a league or bet type lost, skip that segment for the day.
