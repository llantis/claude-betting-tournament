# Lessons: sonnet-high (double elimination)
_Written after round 2 (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-23)._

## Where I went wrong
- Round 2 ended in two straight losses on NBA big-underdog spreads (Wizards +10.5 at 1.93, Bucks +15.5 at 1.92). Both reasons were the same unverified story, "big spreads are inflated, backdoor covers", and I did zero research on either.
- All 8 of my bets (across both rounds) rested on price-only reasoning. None used injuries, rest, lineup news or line movement. I claimed 52-53% on bets that needed 51-52%, a 1-point "edge" that is noise.
- Underdogs at 2.00 or longer went 0-2 (-$9.39). They were my largest stakes (about $4.70 each) and my stated confidence was 46-48%, below break-even in effect. The Vikings +3 reason was only "even money beats -120".
- Overall I won 50% against 51% implied, so my picks had no edge over the market.

## What worked
- Favorites under 2.00: 4-2, ROI +55%. This is only 6 bets, so it is a hint, not proof.
- NCAAF big-underdog spreads went 2-0, again too small to trust.
- Overall calibration was fine (51% stated, 50% actual). My problem was having no edge, not overconfidence.

## Rules for my future bets
1. Do real research before every bet. Check injuries and lineups, rest and back-to-backs, and line movement. If I cannot name a specific piece of information the price ignores, I have no edge, so pick the lowest-variance option (rule 3).
2. Never bet "large spread is inflated" or "backdoor cover" as a reason on its own. It went 0-2 in round 2 and is unproven.
3. After a loss, when a second loss eliminates me, pick the option with the highest true win probability that is still at 1.80 or longer. Prefer favorites and short spreads priced 1.80-1.95. Do not take underdog moneylines or any price above 2.00 on those days.
4. Do not take moneyline underdogs at 2.00 or longer, and do not take three-way soccer moneylines, unless I have a concrete, researched edge. Even then, cap confidence at 50%.
5. Cap confidence at 55%. State confidence only 1-3 points above break-even (1/price) unless I have concrete news behind it. If I cannot justify even that, pick the cleaner favorite.
6. Do not repeat the same bet (team and line) just because it won earlier. The Wizards +10.5 win on 10-28 said nothing about 11-09.
7. Avoid bets whose only support is a rule-of-thumb about a bet type, with no game-specific research.
