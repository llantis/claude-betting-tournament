# Lessons: fable-low (double elimination)
_Written after round 2 (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-23)._

## Where I went wrong
- 10 bets over two rounds: 5-5, ROI -19%. Confidence 52%, implied 52%, actual 50%. I have shown no edge over the market.
- Round 2: 5 of 7 bets said "no research done", including both bets that knocked me out (Texas A&M +1.5 at 1.82, Packers -2.5 at 1.85 all-in). Those five went 2-3.
- My three biggest stakes all lost (Dallas +5.5 -$3.14, Texas A&M -$2.90, Packers -$2.89). All were unresearched "market-shaded side" picks.
- Bets under 2.00: 3-4, -$5.62. I paid 1.82-1.85 for about 52% sides; a win adds little, a loss costs the same.
- All-in bets after a loss: 1-2. "Safest on the slate" at 1.80-1.85 is still a coin flip.
- Claimed edges (56%, 58%) went 1-1; the 58% Knicks ML at 1.80 ended round 1.

## What worked
- Peak $6.27 from three straight wins, two at 1.94-2.02 (Portland twice). Bets at 2.00+ went 2-1, +$1.62. Three bets is a hint, not proof.
- Honest confidence near the no-vig number kept calibration close (52% stated vs 50% actual).

## Rules for my future bets
1. Never submit a pick with "no research done". Every day, check injuries, starting QB or goalie, and rest for at least the three candidate games before choosing.
2. Start from the no-vig probability (use both sides' prices). Confidence may exceed it by at most 3 points, and only with a named, verified fact (confirmed absence, backup starter). Never state above 56%.
3. With no verified fact, confidence equals the no-vig number, and I pick the lowest-vig option: both sides priced 1.90-2.05. Do not take 1.80-1.87 on shading alone.
4. Take a price below 1.90 only when a verified fact favours that side and the other side is 2.00 or longer.
5. All-in day (after a loss): research is mandatory. Pick the highest no-vig win probability among sides where the news supports, or at least does not contradict, the pick. If news is against every such side, take the next one down.
6. After a win (half stake): same rules. Do not reach for prices above 2.20 to rebuild faster.
7. No moneyline underdogs above 2.20; no NHL moneylines on goalie stats alone.
8. Football spreads: check the confirmed starting QB first. Being near a key number (3, 7) is not a reason on its own.
9. Don't bet the same team two days running just because it won yesterday.
10. The reason must name the fact and the no-vig number. If it cannot, say confidence equals no-vig.
