## Analysis (opus-high, bankroll round 1)

There was no earlier lessons file, so this is the first one. Rules: start $10, minimum bet $1, minimum daily stake $1, at most 8 picks a day.

**Bankroll path.** I went from $10 up to a peak of $15.35 on 10-31. From 11-01 to 11-08 I fell to $1.72 (−$13.63 in 8 days, 3 wins in 16 bets). After that I bet heavy favourites at 1.03–1.25 with 50–100% of the bankroll. That stretch went 11-2, but the two losses (Denver 1.25 for −$1.77, Penn State 1.03 for −$1.50) cancelled every win. I was out on 11-21. I replayed the late stretch at a flat $1 a day, and it still busts on 11-21. So the round was really lost from 11-01 to 11-08.

**Where the money went.**
- **Underdogs priced 2.05 or more:** 8 bets, 1 win, −$9.36. That is almost all of my −$10. Priced 3.00 or more: 0-4.
- **Soccer:** 0-6, −$8.00. The reasons were stories like "strong at home", "top Serie A side" and "historically match up well".
- **NFL:** 1-6, −$7.00. On 11-01 I lost three NFL bets for −$5.00 in one day (33% of the bankroll).
- **Coin-flip spreads and totals (prices 1.85–2.05):** 20 bets, 9-11, −$2.13. That is roughly the bookmaker's margin, so no edge. Reasons like "big spreads tend to…", "historically…" and "brand overrating" were not real edges.
- **Stake sizes:** on most early days I staked 27–51% of the bankroll across 2–3 bets with no edge. My biggest stakes (over $2) went 3-3 for −$1.86. The two largest underdog stakes ($2.50 each, at 3.10 and 2.85) both lost.

**Calibration.**
- Overall I said 60%, won 54%, and the prices implied 61%.
- Bets where I said under 50%: I said 36%, won 11% (1-9, −$11.36).
- Bets where I said 50–54%: I won 44%.
- Bets where I said 60% or more: I said 82% and won 82%, because I was just copying the market. They made only +$0.24.

**What worked (all small samples, so hints only):**
- Favourites priced 1.30–1.80 with a specific reason (injury news, line movement, a pitching mismatch): 2-1, +$1.78.
- Totals: 3-2, +$2.13.
- NBA: 14-4, +$2.21.
- I had the best Brier score in the field (0.186) and lasted longest (out on 11-21).

**Main takeaway.** All four players went bust, so simply surviving would have won. Each day costs about the bookmaker's margin times the stake. Without a proven edge, the winning approach is to stake the $1 minimum and avoid the large swings that wiped me out.

# Lessons: opus-high (bankroll)
_Written after round 1 (2026-10-22 to 2026-11-21)._

## Where I went wrong
- Underdogs priced 2.05+ went 1-7 for −$9.36, almost my whole −$10. Priced 3.00+: 0-4.
- Soccer went 0-6 (−$8.00) and NFL 1-6 (−$7.00). The reasons were stories ("strong at home", "historically", "brand overrated"), not edges.
- Coin-flip spreads and totals (1.85–2.05) went 9-11, −7%. I had no edge, only the bookmaker's margin.
- I staked 27–51% of the bankroll a day on bets with no edge. Peak $15.35 on 10-31 fell to $1.72 by 11-08. On 11-01 alone I lost $5 on three NFL bets.
- When I stated under 50% confidence, I won 1 of 9.
- Below $3, betting heavy favourites (1.03–1.25) went 11-2 and still busted me: one loss wipes out 7–30 wins.

## What worked
- Survival: all four players busted, and lasting longest put me first. Bankroll left at the end is what wins.
- Confidence anchored to the no-vig market price was well calibrated (82% stated, 82% won), but it made no money.
- Favourites at 1.30–1.80 with concrete news went 2-1 (+$1.78). Totals went 3-2. Both are hints only (<20 bets).

## Rules for my future bets
1. Default day: exactly one bet, $1.00 stake (the minimum). Total daily stake never above 15% of bankroll.
2. Stake above $1 only if I can cite a specific fact (injury or lineup news after the line opened, or a better price than other books), and keep it ≤10% of bankroll. "Tend to", "historically", "strong at home", "brand" do not qualify.
3. No bet priced above 2.50. Nothing above $1 on any bet priced 2.00+.
4. No soccer bets unless soccer is the only thing on the slate; then $1 on the lowest-margin option.
5. NFL: $1 maximum per bet, at most one NFL bet per day.
6. Never bet when my confidence is under 45%. Confidence = no-vig market probability, plus at most 3 points for a cited fact.
7. On a $1 default day, prefer a favourite priced 1.15–1.60, or a total, with the lowest bookmaker margin. Avoid 1.02–1.08.
8. If bankroll drops 25% below its peak, go to $1 a day, one bet, until it recovers.
9. Below $3 bankroll: stake exactly $1. Never raise the stake just to "keep $1 if it loses".
